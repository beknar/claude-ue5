# The caves rebuilt with the Hyper kit (`945`, `RD/RDCave16.cpp`; the list of 2026-11-22, `docs/improvements16.txt`, phases 8 and 9; `docs/levels/caves.md`)

The cave level (`caves`) with the generator's `bCave16`: `Plan` hands the set to `PlanCave16` (the kit's rooms, tunnels between their openings, portals to the borders), the shell is `CaveShell16` (the kit's meshes, built in play only), `Furnish` is `CaveFurnish16`. Everything the game reads stays tiles (`Type`, `Owner`, `Rooms`, `CaveZ16`).

- **Measure the kit's pieces before laying them**: its rooms are open at the top, its tunnels are walls and a roof with a partial floor (the example map stands them on a stretched cube), its caps close a room's unused openings (on the opening's arrow), a tunnel leaves an opening along its arrow. `945 study` / `measure` read them; trace a mesh from inside its cavity (from above a tunnel's roof the ray meets the roof's back).
- **A tunnel is a wall**: it splits the set. Joins before portals; refuse any laid line that cuts another tunnel's mesh, a room's box it does not join, a cap's ground, or bends tighter than its half-width; try again rather than shrink a keep-out (shrunk, tunnels ran inside each other).
- **A bent kit mesh folds**: the small tunnel (2 to 3 m walkable) closed up on every bend and is drawn by none; round corners to twice the half-width.
- **Tiles meet only through openings**: a tunnel's tiles touch its rooms' only along the opening's own line (a 4-joined chain 6 m in to 6 m out); beside the opening the wall stands (a path there held her on a ledge), and a distance rule refused every tile of a diagonal opening.
- **Runtime components**: attach, set the relative transform, then register; a static component cannot be moved once registered in play, and a world transform set before attaching becomes the offset.
- **The test (`868 cave16`)**: walk every room along `FindPathWorld` steering 3 m ahead along the path (aiming at the next point cut bends into walls); rays from the tunnels' lines for holes; a capsule along each tunnel, blocked only if 1 and 2 m to either side are blocked too; `CaptureAbove16` (the set from 7 m over its floor, unlit) shows crossings and caps at a glance.
- **Phase 9's dressing is the game's, measured from the kit's PCG**: the graphs need the kit's spline Blueprints; `945 dressing` counts the example map's instances by mesh (a PCG output can be listed twice: count each component once). Containers keep 2 tiles off any opening (one there cut the only path in). The lights are the shell's (laid in play, taken down with it): lights made in the furnishing doubled at every restart.
- **A set made in play**: the plan a try a frame, the tunnels' pieces within the frame's budget, the collision from `945 collide`'s reduced copies (a bent piece's collision is cooked synchronously, from the mesh's triangles: 3 s for a set). Measure it as she meets it: clear the start set first (tier 2 opens then), stand by the border, time every frame.
- **Phase 10's draws**: a new item kept to the caves must leave the dungeons' draws as they were: `CaveItemAllowed16` takes it out of the draw's sums and picks both. An item two regions share is left out only if the cave's region does not name it. A run's restart destroys the piles: the record of the veins laid (`CaveNodesLaid16`) is reset with them, or the next run lays none. A buff's kind is an effect kind appended to `ERDItemKind` (Hidden), its words and chip in `RDList14e.cpp`.
- **PR #117's review**: an item kept to the caves must be kept out of every list of things, not only the containers' draw: the stores' stock (`TownStock`) and the special rooms' prizes (`RDM2TierItems`) read the items on their own. A recipe's station by kind (`RecipeStationOf`) sends any Healing item to the alchemist's, and the brewers' rule refuses it to most heroes: a buff carried on a Healing item made elsewhere is named (`AnvilMade16`). A guard against a lasting harm is applied where the harm is dealt each tick, not where it begins. The cave rooms' footprints are reset per room (`CaveFurnish16`), as the castle's Furnish does: kept, every restart laid fewer containers.
- **Phase 11's sockets**: a thing that stays with a piece of her gear is kept by the gear's name and survives what the gear survives: the death drop keeps her sockets (her gear comes back with her pile), only a new game's `ClearItems` empties them, and the carry and the save hold them. A Hidden effect kind is not in Python's `RDItemKind`: a test reads it through a getter of its own (`GetClimateGuard16`).
- **PR #118's review**: a bonus of one weapon's is read by that weapon's index (`GetSocketDamage16(Index)`), not from the weapon in her hands: every weapon's and spell's damage, the HUD's and autoplay's choices read `GetDamageOf(i)`. A thing kept with a piece of gear goes back when the gear leaves her: found weapons leave only through `DropWeaponAt`, items through `RemoveCarried`. Autoplay's potion pick takes any Healing item: one that heals nothing must be left out by its amount.
- **Phase 12's side tunnels**: a set's tunnels are a tree (the loops are tried after the portals and seldom route: 1 set in 13 had one at a loop share of 0.6), so a side tunnel is laid on purpose, from its own stream: a tunnel to nowhere from a free opening, capped. Its end must lie clear of the room's box for a few tiles: an end short of the straight leg out of the opening turned the line back on itself, the rounding gave no number, and the sampling grew until the editor ran out of memory (`Dig` now refuses a line that is not finite). What blocks a set's way must never hold a hero back from clearing it: the cache behind rubble is not a chest of the set's progress. A thing that blocks must look like it: three loose rocks before an unseen box read as a way past (the first picture).
- **A weapon from another weapon**: a found weapon's items come in bands (plain to Divine, one item each with its tier); a copy is made band by band where the map has them (`martial_arts` has only the plain). Another mesh goes on the like's grip by the two meshes' frames (`854`'s measure).
- **PR #119's review**: a rule on a weapon (mended only by its own things) holds at every way it can be mended: the repair items, the anvil (`MendAtAnvil`) and the town smith (`RepairCost`, `TownRepair`). A record named in a header and the docs is nothing until something writes it (the lost blade's caves). A test of "not roused" needs a control that is roused: the quieted monsters of other tests drop their alert on their next tick, so the lightless trial runs unquiet with the start set's monsters killed and reads the alert every 0.1 s.
- **Phase 13's water**: a stretch is under water only between its ends: a point within the tunnel's half-width of the stretch's first or last point but beyond it along the tunnel is dry (the first test read the dry tunnel 4 m before the water as under it, and neither the slowdown nor the refill could be seen). The packs' cave waters are depth-faded: between a tunnel's walls they showed nothing; the engine's plane is one-sided: the water's face is laid both ways up. A worn set's look is its material on every slot of each worn mesh, and `869` gives that material the skeletal-mesh usage.
- **PR #120's review**: a new way to lose hit points goes through `LoseHealth` (the pact, `DamageTaken`) and marks the hurt (`LastHurtTime`, or regeneration runs on); a thing laid "one a cave" is held to one a visit and recorded once taken (the drowned piece had been laid in every flooded set); a stage that pictures every new item reads every list of them (the armour's icons were left out); a translucent two-sided material is laid once.
- **Phase 14's charm slot is a ring kind**: a slot of its own would have needed the inventory, the save, the carry and the death drop each taught; a ring kind (`Charm`) has them all. The ring code reads its words from arrays by kind (`What[3]`, `Who[3]`): a new kind is answered before those reads (`WearRing`, `DescribeItem`). A minimap mark of a new kind needs a number under the bosses' (7 on, `MistMark`) and a branch in each of the three drawings (the minimap and two big maps).
- **PR #121's review**: what a worn thing changes is refreshed when it comes off too (the crown's slow stayed when it was taken off, and put on again slowed her no more: a tick that stops "when nothing is worn" before its refresh); a game-long record is emptied by `NewGame` and before a save's load (a save made before the record holds none, and the tagged load keeps the session's); a lower top taken off gives no hit points back; a bonus on gold is on gold she finds (`AddFoundGold16`), never what she is paid or sells for; a share "on average" is the whole part and the fraction by chance; a "best of each kind" picker keeps to the kinds it knows (the final showcase's rings).
- **Phase 15 (fossils, tablets, paintings, the token)**: a container holds one thing (`MaxItemsPerContainer` 1), so a draw that waits for room (`Out.Num() < Max`) never fires: the tablets came out 0 in 144 containers until the tablet was a thing besides. Measure a draw's share over several session seeds (`SetSessionSeed` with `LootListOf`, the seed put back), not one seed's few containers. A decal on the kit's lumpy rock is a smear: take a wall only where a grid of traces finds it flat. A decal's picture has its top toward its -Y (`MakeFromXY(-Normal, -Up)`): with Z up the figures lay on their sides, with +Y up they stood on their heads; a drawn mark at a known corner (the sun at the top left) tells a picture's way up and its side in one look. A picture cut from a 256 px icon and scaled up is soft blobs on a wall: draw a decal at its size. What F names and what F takes ask the same rule (`PaintingFirst16`: the painting before a pile only when it is nearer), or a vein's pile at the wall's foot took F from the painting the prompt named. M in a town opens the town's map: a test of the wilds' map stands her out of the town (as the contracts').
- **PR #122's review**: a thing F reaches is weighed against everything F reaches (the nearest door or container, piles, anvils, tables), at F's own reach, never one kind alone, and laid clear of them where it can be (the painting 4 m from its set's doors, containers and anvils); a draw of its own is only "its own" if what it draws weighs nothing in every other draw (a spell tablet copied from a tome kept the tome's weight and joined the containers' and the libraries' shelves' draws; the shelves had let the caves' inks out since phase 11); a recipe item locks what it teaches on every map: teach only what is made, never what is found (the Echo's tablet took the Echo out of every cave's draw); a key that names no place (a test's "cave", the package's "cave:go") marks nothing, never Brokenspire's square; the captain's quest places are listed as ruins (ids from `QuestIdBase`); a name the data drops is retired by the stage (`retired`), or the old thing stays on every map; a check of "applied once" counts the applying, not deduplicated results.
