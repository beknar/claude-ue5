## How the models are rigged

| Rig | Which packs | In UE5 |
|---|---|---|
| UE4-mannequin skeleton (305 models) | Almost every Bugrimov human or humanoid | Easiest. UE5 mannequin animations retarget onto them automatically, and their clips are interchangeable. |
| 3ds Max CAT rigs | Cartoon Zombies, Parasites, Baby in Capsule, some Fantasy Animals | Play their own clips; retargeting other animations needs manual setup |
| Maya AdvancedSkeleton | Creatures Mega Pack | Same as above |
| First-person arms + weapon rigs | All FPS packs | Play their own clips |
