new improvements for the mistward castle level
summoner design:
use the Summoner4.glb as the model for the summoner, put her in the mistward_castle level in the heroes to choose menu and when 
she is chosen, can be played with the following capabilities:
can only know 2 spells and has half the mana that the mage does: fire bolt and dark fire, doing the exact same damage and 
the same visual effects, can only use dagger weapons which for her can never be enchanted
the summoner can use the same equipment that mages can
every time she defeats that tier's boss, she gains the next tier of summoning spell, plus whatever treasure that boss drops
she starts with one summoning spell: Summon Tier 1, which summons the Soldier at the cost of half her mana, control 2 of them
at Tier 2, she summons the Gunner, and can summon 3 of them.  mana cost for summoning one is half her mana
at Tier 3, she summons the Orc 05, and she can summon 4 of them.  mana cost for summoning one is half her mana
at Tier 4, she summons the Alien Warrior 05, and she can summon 5 of them.  mana cost for summoning one is half her mana
at Tier 5, she summons the Spider, and she can summon 5 of them.  mana cost for summoning one is half her mana
each tier has different spell casting motions

any piece of equipment can be broken down at an anvil
creatures can now be harvested for crafting parts when they are killed for these parts:
	Any Tiers: Coarse Hide, Light Leather, Cured Leather Strip, Epoxy, Glue, Wood Glue, Oils
	Tier 1-5: Faint Essence
	Tier 2-5: Dim Essence
	Tier 3-5: Bright Essence
	Tier 4-5: Brilliant Essence
	Tier 5: Radiant Essence

Decisions for when you want it built

1. Cooldowns. I'd have a scroll share its spell's cooldown, so a stack of free Fireballs can't be fired back to back.
2. The great tomes' cost. A scroll of Null Sanctum or Apocalypse skips their 220 / 300 mana entirely. That's what "no mana" means, but those two might be better at 6 Radiant.
3. Drop rates. Monsters don't respawn, so a run's drops are limited. The 50 % part chance and the recipe counts are a first guess. The test should count the kills in one generated tier and check that its recipes can actually be made.
