# The caves (`caves`)

The cave complex under every landmark tower of the wilderness: the user's list of 2026-11-20 ([improvements15.txt](../improvements15.txt), item 23: "a cave complex ... of the same Tier that the region is coded for"), its plan's ninth phase ([improvements15_plan.md](../improvements15_plan.md)). The user's choice: every landmark tower holds a cave entrance, one cave level serves them all, its tier set by where the entrance stands.

## What it is

- **The entrance**: the wilderness's landmark towers (a broken tower 182 m high and 37 m across at a fixed place in each 6 km square of the open lands, since the list of 2026-11-14) have a round rock arch 13 m across at their foot, between two of the five buttresses, a dark mouth inside it. F before it ("go down into the cave") takes her down.
- **One level, every tower's cave**: `caves` is a Save As copy of `mistward_castle` (its heroes, balance, loot, traps, special rooms and bosses). The tower's 6 km cell is the cave's key (`cave:<i>_<j>`: its tiers opened, its first set's chests and its bosses kept under it, as each city's dungeon keeps its own), and the region's tier at the tower (`FRDWildWorld::TierAt`, the roads' tiers) is every tier of the level: its three tiers of sets open in order as the castle's do, each one the same game tier (`DungeonTierBase` the tier less one, `DungeonTierCap` the tier). The level reads both from the game instance as play begins (`ApplyCave15`); a save made in a cave keeps them.
- **Its looks**: rock walls, rough ground and rock ceilings (the Cave pack's rock and ground materials, darkened, on the castle's walls', floors' and ceilings' boxes), the vaults over the rooms in the same rock, stalagmites for the halls' columns, a rock arch round each doorway, rock faces, stalagmites, boulders and stones by the walls, the light tinted cold and dim; each tier a floor 4.5 m below the last, joined by ramps (the catacombs' `bTierFloors`). The first hall is a cavern: none of the castle's carpet, dais, throne, banners or chandeliers.
- **Its monsters**: the wilds' kinds whose wild tier band holds the cave's tier (the nearest tier below with any, else), none whose name marks a person with a firearm or a machine (`SourceArt/caves/cave15.json`'s `exclude`); their strength the tier's.
- **The castle's own systems are off**: no prison cells, no arena, no key chain (no key needed to enter, none dropped), no final boss, and a tier's boss gives the tier's loot and gold but none of the castle's unique rewards (no slash, no chi attack, no later dungeon's ability).
- **The way back**: the Escape menu's "Return to the Broken Tower" puts her 6 m before the mouth she went in by, facing away from the tower.

## How it was built

- `935 saveas` (the copy; the editor quits), `935` (the level: brokenspire's monsters with their wild bands copied as text, the Cave theme's pieces and props, the switches, the start section built again and saved), `935 wild` (on `brokenspire`: the cave level's path and the mouth's pieces and placing). Data: `SourceArt/caves/cave15.json`. C++: `RD/RDList15h.cpp` (the mouth, the way in, the level's key and tier, its kinds), the cave hall in `RDSection.cpp`, no cells or arena in `RDSection.cpp` / `RDMistward2.cpp`, the boss's rewards in `RDDungeonGenerator.cpp`.
- **The pack's rock walls and ground slabs are blobs**: laid one a wall segment or a floor tile they left the void showing between them (the first pictures: black between round floor slabs). The walls, floors and ceilings are boxes of the castle's pieces' sizes in the pack's rock and ground; the rock walls stand along the walls as props.
- **The world-aligned rock projects from above**: on the walls' faces it was a flat grey. The walls and ceilings take the pack's rock wall material (by UV, its tint darkened), the floors its world-aligned ground.
- **The pack's arch is a quarter round**: the mouth is that piece and its mirror; its foot is at its own X -624 (placed the other way, the two halves stood as horns). Inside it, a dark half disc (the engine's cylinder on its side, half in the ground, in an unlit near-black).
- **The tower's buttresses stand at 0, 72, ... degrees in Unreal as in Blender**: at +36 degrees the first mouths stood inside a buttress (the test stood her on its top, 28 m up); the gap is at -36 of the generator's frame (`CaveGapDeg15`).

## Rebuilt with the Hyper kit (the list of 2026-11-22, phase 8)

The user's list of 2026-11-22 ([improvements16.txt](../improvements16.txt); its plan [improvements16_plan.md](../improvements16_plan.md), phases 8 and 9) asks for the caves rebuilt with the Hyper Cave Building Toolkit, "not the same as the dungeon procedural generation, not a dungeon with cave textures". Phase 8 lays and draws them; phase 9 dresses them (the kit's PCG, torches, god rays, monsters' places, containers, the boss, rubble).

- **A planner of its own** (`RD/RDCave16.cpp`, the generator's `bCave16`): a set is the kit's rooms, not the castle's rooms and hallways. The start set has the large room on its middle tile (where the level's player start and the way out stand); 4 to 7 rooms a set, by tier, are drawn from the kit's small, medium and large rooms, turned by quarter turns, standing where their whole box and 6 tiles round it are free; some a level lower (4.28 m, the kit's stairs' rise). A tree of joins (nearest first) and a fifth more as loops link them; each join is a tunnel from one room's opening to the other's. A portal on each side of the set (a fixed row, chosen by a hash of the border, so the two sets of a border agree) is a tunnel from a room to the border; one that finds no way is closed at the border by the kit's cap, so the set beyond ends there in rock. Two rooms of different levels are joined through the kit's stairs where they and both their tunnels fit, else the tunnel slopes. An opening no tunnel uses is closed by the kit's cap.
- **A tunnel**: routed over the tile grid (A* round every room's box and every other tunnel's mesh, the cost wound by a noise field), pulled straight where its tiles see ahead, its corners rounded (a radius of twice the tunnel's half-width, 13 m at least), then laid as the kit lays it: the tunnel mesh bent along the curve in pieces of 17.7 m overlapping by 2.5 m (spline-mesh components), the kit's floor strip of its size under it, and its height eased from one end's floor to the other's. A line that would cut another tunnel or a room it does not join, bend tighter than its half-width, or run through a cap's ground is refused, and the join is tried with other openings. A plan that leaves a room no tunnel reaches is laid again (each try its own stream; the best of 8 is kept: no room cut off, then the most portals).
- **Everything the game reads stays tiles**: the rooms' walkable tiles and floor heights are the kit's as traced on its meshes (`945 measure`), a tunnel's tiles are those within 2.15 m of its line and are a Junction room of their own (so the monsters' tile paths, the maps, the tiers' progress and the saves work as before), and a tunnel meets its rooms' tiles only along the opening's own line (beside it the room's wall stands).
- **Built in play only**: the start set's shell is never saved in the level (the editor's build of it lays the plan and no meshes, and saves its lights); every set lays its meshes as it is built. A set made in play plans a try a frame and lays its tunnels' pieces within the frame's budget; the census and the start set plan at once (the same streams: the same cave).
- **Stages**: `945 study` reads the kit's example map (each room's mesh, size, openings (its Blueprint's arrows), roof and god-ray points, the stairs' ends, the tunnels' meshes and their pieces' length and overlap, the cap) into `SourceArt/caves/hyper16.json`; `945 measure` traces each room's walkable tiles and floor heights and each tunnel's floor on its mesh; `945 level` writes the kit and `bCave16` onto the cave level's generator and builds its start set again.

### What the kit taught

- **Its rooms are open at the top** (a roof piece with a hole closes them) and **its tunnels are walls and a roof only**: the example map stands them on a stretched cube for ground. Without a floor she fell through; the kit's floor strips (4, 8 and 12 m wide) go under each tunnel, 90 % of its width, 10 cm under its own partial floor.
- **Its caps close a room's unused openings** (placed on the opening's arrow, its yaw), not a tunnel's end; a tunnel starts on an opening's arrow and leaves along it.
- **Its small tunnel is drawn by none**: its walkable way is 2 to 3 m wide, and bent it closed up: she stuck on its walls in every small tunnel and never in a medium one. Bent tighter than about its own half-width, any tunnel folds its inner wall into the way.
- **A tunnel is a wall**: four portal tunnels laid before the rooms' joins cut the set into quarters the joins could not cross; the joins come first.
- **A static component cannot be moved once it is registered in play**: the rooms all stood at the set's corner; their place is set before registering.
- **The guide video** (TQdGCKnO6lc) could not be watched: the browser extension was not connected and the page's text gave only its title; the example map was studied instead.

## Dressed (the list of 2026-11-22, phase 9)

- **The kit's PCG dressing, scattered by the game**: the kit's PCG graphs run on its own spline Blueprints (a spline component and the Blueprint's variables), which the game's tunnels (bent meshes laid by the C++) do not have, and running PCG in a packaged game would need the plugin enabled and its graphs cooked. - **The boss**: a tier's boss stands in the deepest explored room (its floor the lowest, then the farthest from her).
- **Not built here**: the rubble across side tunnels comes with the miner's pick (phase 12), which clears it: laid now it would only shut ways; the way in and out is the castle's (the start room's teleporter; the mouth under the tower), the maps the tiles'.

## The caves' materials (the list of 2026-11-22, phase 10)

The list's "treasure types that can be found in caves instead of the things that can be found in dungeons", its crafting materials. Their data is `SourceArt/caves/treasure16.json`'s `phase10`; `945 items` writes them on every map of the game (each copied from a "like" of its kind, with its own numbers, buff, picture and words; its recipe where it has one), `945 icons` gives every map their 22 pictures (painted on the InvokeAI server, `SourceArt/caves/icons16`). C++ `RD/RDCave16b.cpp`.

- **Found only in caves**: the caves' own items (`CaveItems16`, on every map) are drawn by a container only in a cave, and there a cave's gear, rings, tomes and scrolls come from them alone (none yet: they come with phases 11 to 14); its potions, food, repair items and the Angel Tokens' materials are drawn as anywhere. Outside the caves they are out of the draw's sums and picks both, so a dungeon's draws are what they were.
- **A cave's region**: its tower's biome (kept by the game instance at the mouth, saved) puts it in one of six regions: cold, mountains, volcanic, desert, forest, open. A geode's colour is its region's (blue, white, red, yellow, green, violet), frost quartz is found in the cold lands' and the mountains' caves, sun-glass in the volcanic lands' and the desert's.
- **Veins and growths**: in each kit room, one or two deepiron veins at the wall's foot (a standing rock of the kit's in the Old Mine pack's coal stone: a pack's crystal read as a caged lamp, the kit's iron-trimmed rocks as pale stone) and glowcap and grave-moss growths on its floor, each with a harvest pile (F: "harvest the parts"); a set built again lays none twice, a new run lays them again.
- **The things**: deepiron ore (three make an ingot at an anvil), glowcap, grave-moss, cave pearl (a trade good), bat-wing leather (two make a patch that mends light armour only), crawler chitin (mends medium armour only), blind-fish oil, a giant's femur and a wyrm vertebra (rare, for a named weapon later), amber with an insect and petrified wood (reagents, and collectibles later), frost quartz and sun-glass, six geode shards.
- **What they make**: the darksight draught (two glowcap: for five minutes an 18 m light on her, no torch needed), the grave-moss antivenom (a poison hurts 60 % less for five minutes), the slow mending draught (grave-moss, glowcap and amber or petrified wood: half a percent of her hit points a second for three minutes), the frost-quartz lining and the sun-glass weave (the climate's warm and cool buffs, 50 % for half an hour), the deepiron ingot and the bat-wing patch. Blind-fish oil used makes the torch and the ankh burn twice as long for twenty minutes.
- **Not here yet**: the deepiron anvil recipes for weapons and armour one tier above the smith's come with the cave weapons and armour (phases 12 and 13); the geodes are cut into inks and socket stones in phase 11; the bones' named weapon in phase 12; amber's collectible in phase 15.

## Geodes, inks and socket stones (the list of 2026-11-22, phase 11)

The list's "crystal geode shards, cut at the librarian's table into scroll inks or socket stones; a colour per region". `treasure16.json`'s `phase11`, written by `945 items` (and its pictures by `945 icons`) on every map of the game; C++ `RD/RDCave16c.cpp`.

- **Cut at the librarian's table**: one geode shard makes an ink of its colour, two a socket stone.
- **Inks**: any ink stands in for a scroll recipe's reagent (the part that is not its essence: a venom sac, a horn shard...), in every one of the 36 scroll recipes.
- **Sockets**: every weapon (not magic, chi or a scroll's) and every set of armour has one socket. A stone used goes into the weapon in her hands if its socket is empty, else the armour she wears, else it takes the place of the one set longer ago and the old stone goes back to her pack. A red stone strengthens its own weapon's blows (and, in her armour, all of them); gear sold, broken down or broken gives its stone back. Autoplay never uses a stone (or anything else that heals nothing) as a potion. A stone works while its weapon is in her hands or its armour on her; the sockets go with her between the levels and the save, and stay with her gear when she dies (it comes back with her pile). The inventory says what a gear's socket holds.
- **The colours** (a region's own, as its geodes):

| Stone | Region | While it works |
|---|---|---|
| Red | volcanic | her blows 10 % stronger |
| White | mountains | a blow on her 8 % less (beside the armour's own share) |
| Blue | cold | the cold comes on 40 % more slowly (as a warm meal's buff, beside it) |
| Yellow | canyons and savannahs | the heat 40 % more slowly |
| Green | forests and wetlands | 0.2 % of her hit points back a second |
| Violet | open lands and coasts | 8 % faster |

## Limits

- (Before the list of 2026-11-22) the rooms were the castle's rooms in rock; with the planner they are the kit's (above).
- The dressing is the game's scatter of the kit's meshes, not the kit's PCG graphs (above); the hanging sorrel at 15 % of the kit's density.
- The torches burn the castle's torch flame: the kit's own (`NS_2DGasIntro`) needs the NiagaraFluids plugin, which the project does not have (the package's cook said so).
- A portal that finds no tunnel is capped: a set has 3 or 4 of its 4 (six seeds), so a neighbour can be reached by fewer ways than its sides.
- The kit's stairs are laid only where they and both their tunnels fit whole, else a tunnel slopes between levels; the measured start set has none (how often a set has stairs is not measured yet).
- Every tower's cave has the same first hall (the saved start section); the sets beyond it are new each visit, as the castle's are.
- The tower's top is a plain round slab in the tower's own stone, stretched over the broken top (no parapet seen: a hidden rim holds her), and the lift is a door's F, no moving cage.
- The way in is the mouth's F, or the map's travel to a mouth a fragment attuned (phase 16); the map marks the caves a studied painting showed her (phase 15); a contract whose beast fled leads into one.
