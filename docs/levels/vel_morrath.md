# Vel Morrath

A cathedral city built over catacombs that go deeper than anyone has mapped (the user's spec of 2026-11-06, [docs/vel_morrath.txt](../vel_morrath.txt)). The city is one of the wilderness's towns on the `brokenspire` level, rebuilt as a full-size holy city north-east of Brokenspire and inland; the catacombs under its cathedral are a level of their own, `vel_morrath`, five tiers deep, each a floor below the last.

- **Maps:** the city on `/Game/RandomDungeon/Maps/brokenspire` (streamed in the wilds as she comes, like the other towns); the catacombs `/Game/RandomDungeon/Maps/vel_morrath` (a Save As copy of `mistward_castle`: the same heroes, balance, monsters, loot, traps, special rooms and bosses)
- **Played from:** the cathedral's stair down (walked into at the choir's far wall); the catacombs' Escape menu's "Return to Vel Morrath" brings her back before the cathedral's door. `vel_morrath` has no menu of its own: played from the editor it starts the generator's `DefaultHero`.
- **C++:** `RD/RDTownPlan.cpp` (the holy plan, `TownPlan` 1: the cathedral's rooms, the plaza, the monastery, the chapels, the graveyard, the extra streets; its furnishing: pews, the altar), `RD/RDHoly.cpp` (the cathedral's shell, its gables, towers and spires; the stair down: `EnterCatacombs`; the way back's name: `WayBackName`), `RD/RDWildWorld.cpp` / `RDWildTown.cpp` (the city's place and size), `RD/RDSection.cpp` (the catacombs' floors and stairs, behind `bTierFloors`), `RD/RDGameInstance.h` (each dungeon's tiers and start loot under its key, the way back: `ReturnAt`, `ReturnTown`)
- **Scripts:** `891_vel_morrath_catacombs.py` (the catacombs level), `892_vel_morrath_city.py` (the city's pieces, people and quests on `brokenspire`); tests `884 holy,descend` and `868 catacombs` (with `-lv vel_morrath`), `888 world,towns`
- **Data:** `SourceArt/vel_morrath/city.json` (the townsfolk and the quests)

## The city

- **Where:** 55 km north and 75 km east of Brokenspire (93 km: the wilderness's tier 4), no sea within 30 km east of it (measured by walking out, `888 world`). Since 2026-11-06; until then a small town 115 km due east, near the coast.
- **Its size and plan:** Brokenspire's size (168 tiles, 504 m a side; the other wild towns are small), built from the town's rules with the holy plan (`FRDWildTown::Plan` 1): the cathedral at the middle with the plaza before it (a fountain, four wayside shrines, four statues on their plinths, the market stalls, the quest board), a second cross street and flank streets, the monastery, three chapels, a temple, the trades (smithy, store, healer, storehouse, tavern, barracks), about 90 homes (few burned or boarded: 4 and 3 of 92 in one test), the graveyard behind the cathedral; no witch's hut.
- **The cathedral** ("The Cathedral of Vel Morrath"), seven rooms: the narthex, the nave (15 m high, pews in rows facing the choir every 2.3 m either side of the aisle, chandeliers), the choir with the altar and its candles, two aisles (a library and a treasury), two bell towers (27 m) with spires. Inside, Castle of Eternal Mist's blind arches; outside, its columned stone and arched windows, gabled roofs. The stair down stands at the choir's far wall under a crypt arch, with the teleporter's glow.
- **The houses:** House Forge's stone set in Castle of Eternal Mist's pale arch stone ("Holy.*", house style 4), their roofs and doors their own. Every building has rooms, a door that opens and containers (371 containers, 136 doors in one test; the cathedral 55 containers).
- **Its people** (34 kinds, `city.json`): the functional ones, each with the part of Brokenspire's notable of that role: Archbishop Severin Aurel at the cathedral's door (the elder's part: talk, quests), Archivist Mott (the scholar's), Commander Ysolde Vane of the templars (the captain's), and the trades' keepers (their services: Mother Ilsabet's temple, Garrick Tull's smithy, Ansel the Chandler's store, Sister Brenna the healer, Wardkeeper Holt's storehouse, Matron Gilda's tavern). The others walk, stand, pray and talk: preachers, monks and nuns in the monastery and the nave, pilgrims, beggars, templars, sentries, children, a mourner, a fool, citizens. None of Brokenspire's notables is among them.
- **Its quests:** the archbishop's "The Stair Beneath the Altar" (go down into the catacombs, 30 gold), "Deeper Than the Bells" (reach tier 2, 70), "Where the Maps End" (tier 4, 180); the commander's "Ash and Bone" (25 kills below, 50) and "The Warden Below" (a tier's boss, 90); the archivist's "What the Monks Wrote" (talk to a monk, 15); the board's "Bounty: Clear the Ossuary" (10 kills, 25). They count the catacombs' own counters (`vel_morrath:enter`, `vel_morrath:tier`, `vel_morrath:kills`, `vel_morrath:boss`), never the castle's.
- **Frame rate:** 24 a second at the plaza looking at the cathedral once the city has settled (Brokenspire's square in the same session: 15 to 30); 5 just after the city was built round her. Hiding its lights, any pack's pieces, its people or its effects moved it by 1 to 4.

## The catacombs

- **Floors going down** (the generator's `bTierFloors`): tier 1 (the start set, under the cathedral) at the city's level, each tier `TierFloorDepth` (4.5 m) below the one before: tier 2 at -4.5 m, tier 3 at -9 m, down to tier 5 at -18 m (tiers 1 to 3 measured; 4 and 5 follow the same rule and have not been visited in a test). Every hallway out of a set toward a shallower one climbs to it over its last `StairTiles` (4) tiles before the border, a ramp of 21 degrees under a sloped ceiling (3.9 m of headroom); the set's edge rooms are kept that far in. The tiers open as Mistward Castle's do (half of the tier before cleared), behind the same gratings.
- **Its looks by tier:** tier 1 the old stone crypts (the dungeon's own pieces), tier 2 the crypt stone (HallowedDepths' crypt block walls and slab floors, "Crypt"), tier 3 the buried cathedral's arches (the castle's "Castle"), tier 4 the deep halls older than the city ("Dwarf"), tier 5 all three mixed.
- **Its own record:** its tiers, its start set's loot, its bosses and prisoners and the quests' counters are kept under its key (`DungeonKey` "vel_morrath"), apart from Mistward Castle's. No final boss (Aurorim is the castle's).
- **Her whole state** goes down and comes back with her, as between Brokenspire and the castle (statistics, every thing she carries, what is on her, her summons).

## Rebuild notes

1. `891_vel_morrath_catacombs.py saveas`: saves `mistward_castle` as `vel_morrath` and quits the editor; start the editor again and run `891` (no argument): the Crypt theme's pieces, the tiers' themes, `bTierFloors` and its depth and stairs, its key, title and town, no final boss, no menu; its start section built. The catacombs are a copy of the castle as it was on 2026-11-06: a later stage that writes the castle's heroes, items, monsters or pieces must be run on `vel_morrath` too.
2. `892_vel_morrath_city.py` on `brokenspire`, after `882` / `886` / `890` (which rewrite the "Town." pieces and Brokenspire's people; they keep the city's, which have a town set): the city's houses ("Holy.*"), the cathedral's pieces ("Cathedral.*": walls, windows, floors, ceilings, spires, chandeliers, pews, the altar), the plaza's statues, fountain and shrines, the crypt arch, the catacombs' level (`CatacombsLevel`), its people and quests from `city.json`.
3. The C++ (`RDHoly.cpp`, the holy plan in `RDTownPlan.cpp`, the floors in `RDSection.cpp`): `pipeline\build_cpp.ps1` with the editor closed.
4. The tests above; the castle's (`868`) and Brokenspire's (`884`) own after anything shared.
