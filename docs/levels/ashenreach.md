# Ashenreach

A forge city on the edge of a burned plain, ruled by a forge-guild, whose job is to build weapons, armour and gear; its dungeon is The War Engine, the besieged factory under its Great Foundry (the user's spec of 2026-11-07, [docs/ashenreach.txt](../ashenreach.txt)). The city is one of the wilderness's towns on the `brokenspire` level, rebuilt as a full-size forge city in the continent's middle north of Brokenspire; The War Engine is a level of its own, `war_engine`.

- **Maps:** the city on `/Game/RandomDungeon/Maps/brokenspire` (streamed in the wilds as she comes, like the other towns); The War Engine `/Game/RandomDungeon/Maps/war_engine` (a Save As copy of `mistward_castle`: the same heroes, balance, loot, traps, special rooms and bosses)
- **Played from:** the iron gate at the Great Foundry's furnace hall's far wall (walked into); The War Engine's Escape menu's "Return to Ashenreach" brings her back before the foundry's door. `war_engine` has no menu of its own: played from the editor it starts the generator's `DefaultHero`.
- **C++:** `RD/RDTownPlan.cpp` (the forge plan, `TownPlan` 2, `bForge`: the foundry's rooms, the square's things, the workshops, the siege's posts), `RD/RDForge.cpp` (the foundry's shell, its chimneys, kilns, gate, the casting hall's forge lines; the great anvil, open forges, ore carts and barricade), `RD/RDHoly.cpp` (`EnterCatacombs` / `DescendToCatacombs` / `CurrentDungeonLevel`: each town's own dungeon, the generator's `TownDungeons`), `RD/RDWildWorld.cpp` (the city's place, size and burned plain: `FRDWildTown::Biome`), `RD/RDSection.cpp` (a dungeon's props by its walls: the generator's `ThemeProps`), `RD/RDMonster.cpp` (a kind's own material: `FRDMonsterDef::Material`)
- **Scripts:** `894_ashenreach_city.py` (the city's pieces, effects, people and quests on `brokenspire`, the dungeon's level for the town), `895_war_engine.py` (the dungeon level and its machines), `898_improvements8.py block` (the Warrior's block walk, on `war_engine` too); tests `884 forge,descendwar`, `888 world`
- **Data:** `SourceArt/ashenreach/city.json` (the townsfolk and the quests), `SourceArt/war_engine/monsters.json` (the machines), `SourceArt/wilderness/biomes.json`'s "burned plain"

## The city

- **Where:** 70 km due north of Brokenspire, the continent's middle (as since 2026-11-03); the burned plain over the region north of it and the city's own (measured 6 km north: "burned plain"; 9 km south: "plains").
- **The burned plain:** ash-grey ground (a, b `[60,56,52]`, `[82,74,64]`, a share of mud), charred dead trees (Bone Temple's, Wildlands'), fallen branches, dead weeds, a few dark lava rocks.
- **Its size and plan:** Brokenspire's size (168 tiles, 504 m a side), built from the town's rules with the forge plan (`FRDWildTown::Plan` 2): the Great Foundry at the middle (21 m from it measured) with the square before it (74 m), the processional ways round the foundry, four workshops down the main road and round the square, the trades (smithy, store, healer, storehouse, tavern, barracks, temple, a chapel), about 85 homes (4 burned, 8 boarded of 87 in one test), the graveyard; no witch's hut.
- **The Great Foundry** ("The Great Foundry of Ashenreach"), seven rooms: the gatehouse between the two chimney stacks' bases, the casting hall (14 m high; a forge and its anvil at every other tile down both long walls clear of the side doors' rows (19 hooded forges in the city in ar4 and ar5, 15 when they stood every third tile; pictured across the hall: `ar_casting_side`), an ore cart's rail with two carts), the furnace hall (a brick kiln either side of the iron gate, their fire and glow), a workshop wing down each side (an armoury, the stores), the two stacks (24 m) each with its chimney and smoke. Inside Dwarven Citadel's brick, its floor tiles and chandeliers; outside its brick; gabled roofs of the city's stone.
- **The siege:** a barricade of spiked palisades (War Camp) across the forecourt facing the foundry's door, a gap in its middle; four soldiers of the siege behind it, braziers at its ends, the siege captain and the foundry's guards inside it.
- **The square:** the well, the guild's great anvil on its plinth, an open forge (the castle kit's hooded forge, its anvil, fire, sparks and smoke) in each corner, three ore carts on their rails (Old Mine) on the forecourt's sides, the stalls, the quest board.
- **The houses:** House Forge's stone set in a darkened copy of its own stone (`MI_Forge_SootStone`, "Forge.*", house style 5), their roofs and doors their own. Every building has rooms, a door that opens and containers (496 containers, 132 doors in `test_ar5.json`; the foundry 131 of them).
- **Its people** (30 kinds, `city.json`): Guildmaster Torvald Brask before the barricade (the elder's part: talk, quests), Master Artificer Odile Fenn (the scholar's), Captain Marek Dunholt (the captain's), the trades' keepers (Mother Ashwyn's temple, Hilde Ironhand's smithy, Corvin Slagg's store, Sister Calla the healer, Bram the Weigher's storehouse, Dagny Coalbrook's tavern), the guild's forge hands (`Worker`: their rounds between their workshop's anvil and fire and the square's open forges, 12 in one test), soldiers of the siege, foundry hands who got out (at the gate), sentries, guildsmen on the walls, children, a mourner, a fool, citizens. None of Brokenspire's notables is among them.
- **Its quests** (on its map and over its givers' heads): the guildmaster's "Through the Iron Gate" (go down into The War Engine, 30 gold), "Bank the Second Furnace" (its tier 2, 70), "The Heart of the Engine" (tier 4, 180), each offered once the one before is done; the captain's "Scrap Them" (25 kills below, 50) and, after it, "Break a Governor" (a tier's boss, 90); the artificer's "The Guild's Ledger" (talk to a forge hand, 15); the board's "Bounty: Iron Hounds" (10 kills, 25). They count The War Engine's own counters (`war_engine:enter`, `:tier`, `:kills`, `:boss`).
- **Frame rate:** 22 to 24 a second in the casting hall in an earlier run; 5 to 7 on the square just after the city was built round her (Vel Morrath's plaza read 5 then and 24 settled: not measured settled here). `test_ar5.json` read 6.9 on the square and 7.0 in the casting hall.

## The War Engine

- **Its halls by tier** (the castle's sets spread out by tier, no floors going down; only tier 1's first hall seen in a test): tiers 1 and 2 the foundry's brick (Dwarven Citadel's brick walls and floor tiles, "Foundry"), tier 3 the dwarven forge halls (the castle's "Dwarf"), tier 4 the brick again, tier 5 both mixed; by every room's walls after its furniture the factory's props (`ThemeProps`: rusted gears and chain piles (Junkyard), ore carts (Old Mine), anvils, brick kilns, rusted oil barrels), from a stream of the room's own.
- **Its machines** (`monsters.json`, eleven kinds among the castle's 56, weighted so that about two in three monsters of a tier are machines; 11 of the first hall's 19 in one test): tier 1 the scrap robot and the flayed mechanic; tier 2 the scrap mutant, the rusted robot, the cyber convict; tier 3 the iron golem (the Stone Golem in rusted iron, 1.15 times its size; slows), the cyber soldier (weakens), the mutant trooper (poisons); tier 4 the War Engine mech (Dystopia's blade-armed mech; slows); tier 5 the iron colossus (slows) and the engine mech (weakens), both larger (1.5 and 1.3 times). Those on a UE4 mannequin rig with no fighting clips of their own play the Fantasy Pack's (their skeletons made compatible); all eleven measured playing (their bones moving 1.9 to 55 cm in half a second).
- **Its own record:** its tiers, its start set's loot, its bosses and prisoners and the quests' counters are kept under its key (`DungeonKey` "war_engine"), apart from Mistward Castle's and the catacombs'. No final boss.
- **Her whole state** goes down and comes back with her, as between Brokenspire and the castle.
