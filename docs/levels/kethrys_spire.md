# Kethrys Spire

A mage city clustered round a black tower whose gates have not opened in a century, the lights in its upper windows still moving, with magical effects, laboratories, libraries, study rooms, meditation chambers and stone houses, not a poor city; its dungeon is inside the tower (the user's list of 2026-11-08b, [docs/improvements9.txt](../improvements9.txt), its fourth phase). The city is one of the wilderness's towns on the `brokenspire` level; the tower's dungeon is a level of its own, `kethrys_spire`, the fifth of the chain of dungeons ([the keys](wilderness.md#the-list-of-2026-11-08b-third-part-the-dungeons-keys-and-the-roads-tiers)): its Spire Key is dropped by the Sewers of Graymourne's boss.

- **Maps:** the city on `/Game/RandomDungeon/Maps/brokenspire` (streamed in the wilds as she comes, like the other towns); the tower's dungeon `/Game/RandomDungeon/Maps/kethrys_spire` (a Save As copy of `mistward_castle`: the same heroes, balance, loot, traps, special rooms and bosses)
- **Played from:** the sealed gate in the tower's hall (walked into; without the Spire Key it stays shut and says whose master holds it); the dungeon's Escape menu's "Return to Kethrys Spire" brings her back before the tower's door. `kethrys_spire` has no menu of its own: played from the editor it starts the generator's `DefaultHero`.
- **C++:** `RD/RDTownPlan.cpp` (the mage plan, `TownPlan` 4, `bMage`: the tower at the middle, the libraries, laboratories, study rooms and meditation chambers, the runes and crystal pylons, the posts; well kept: hardly a house burned or boarded), `RD/RDMage.cpp` (the tower's shell: its gatehouse and hall in black stone, its round storeys up to 55 m, crown and cap, the light beam and mist at the top, its violet lights, the upper storeys' moving lights, the sealed gate and its ward; the runes and crystals; the laboratories' cauldrons, tables and potions), `RD/RDSection.cpp` (a meditation chamber's furnishing: its rune circle and candles; a dungeon's lights tinted by the level, `DungeonLightTint`), `RD/RDTown.h` and `RD/RDTownHUD.cpp` (the black tower, the library, the laboratory, the study and the meditation chamber as buildings, the rune and the crystal as spots, on the town's map), `RD/RDWildWorld.cpp` (the city's place and its road to Graymourne)
- **Scripts:** `906_kethrys_city.py` (the city's pieces, effects, people and quests on `brokenspire`, the tower's level for the town), `907_kethrys_spire.py` (the dungeon level, its arcane halls and its casters); tests `884 spire,descendspire` (with `-Light`), `888 world,towns`
- **Data:** `SourceArt/kethrys/city.json` (the townsfolk and the quests), `SourceArt/kethrys_spire/monsters.json` (the casters)

## The city

- **Where:** north-west of Graymourne, inland: placed 45 km south and 70 km west of the port and walked inland until the land is solid (152.4 km north of Brokenspire and 70 km west in the test's game, 83 km from Graymourne, measured). A road joins it to Graymourne.
- **Its size and plan:** Brokenspire's size (168 tiles, 504 m a side), built from the town's rules with the mage plan (`FRDWildTown::Plan` 4): the black tower at the middle (14 m from it, measured), the trades (smithy, store, healer, storehouse, tavern, barracks, temple, a chapel), two libraries, two laboratories, two study rooms and two meditation chambers round the square and down the main road, about 90 homes of House Forge's stone set in a cooled copy of its stone (92 in `test_ks7.json`, 1 burned, 1 boarded), the graveyard. Every building has rooms, a door that opens and containers (332 containers, 127 doors in `test_ks7.json`).
- **The black tower:** a gatehouse (4 by 7 tiles, a guard room) before a great hall (a library's room kind), their walls, floor and ceiling the town castle's in a black copy of their stone (`MI_Spire_Black`); over the hall the tower rises in round storeys (Castle of Eternal Mist's round tower with windows, 2.2 times its size), its crown and its cap, 69.1 m to the top (measured), a beam of light and mist at the top, violet lights inside. **The lights in the upper windows still move**: in the top two storeys two violet lights and their glowing orbs each go round the tower at the windows' line, each storey its own pace and way (14 to 32 degrees a second): four lights, 36 m up and higher, each 5.3 to 5.4 m further round 2 s later (`test_ks7.json`); at night the glow passes over the upper windows (its pictures from the tower's front 1.5 s apart). Put inside the tower they lit nothing that showed: the windows are recesses (`test_ks6.json`'s night picture). The sealed gate (`Spire.Gate`) stands at the hall's far wall with a ward's glow over it, 26 m from the tower's door. It reads on the town's map as "THE BLACK TOWER · sealed for a century".
- **The magic in the streets:** a rune circle on the square (Relics' rune stone with Particle Rings' glow), crystal pylons (an ice crystal glowing on a plinth) at the square's corners and down the main road (10 in one test), the laboratories' cauldrons bubbling (Liquid Surface's magic potion) by their tables of potions; the libraries' rooms are the castle's library room kind (tall wooden cases, candle stands, the scribes among them), and so are the study rooms (smaller: desks, chairs, books and candles, an apprentice at them); a meditation chamber has none of that: a rune circle glowing on the floor in its middle, four small rune stones round it, a ring of candles, a candle stand in each quarter, two apprentices standing still by the circle.
- **Its people** (29 kinds, `city.json`): Archmage Velis Kethran by the tower (the elder's part: talk, quests), Archivist Mirel Ondra (the scholar's), Warden-Captain Iska Thorne (the captain's), the trades' keepers (Mother Selwyn's temple, Hollen the Runesmith's smithy, Quillon Vey's store, Sister Amarah the healer, Oswin the Ledger's storehouse, Bess Lanterne's tavern), Brother Aethel the preacher, the Spire's scribes in the libraries (4 in `test_ks7.json`), alchemists in the laboratories (4), apprentice mages in the streets, the studies and the meditation chambers (11), the wardens of the Spire before the tower (5), guards, watchmen on the walls, children, a widow, Fizzwick the Unbound (the town's fool), citizens.
- **Its quests** (on its map and over its givers' heads): the archmage's "The Gate of a Hundred Years" (go into the tower, 40 gold), "Halls of the Tower" (its tier 2, 90), "The Heart of the Spire" (tier 4, 220), each offered once the one before is done; the warden-captain's "Thin the Wards" (25 kills inside, 60) and, after it, "The Tower's Master" (a tier's boss, 110); the archivist's "The Scribes' Account" (talk to a scribe, 15); the board's "Notice: Wardbreakers Wanted" (10 kills, 30). They count the tower's own counters (`kethrys:enter`, `:tier`, `:kills`, `:boss`).
- **Its roads' tier:** 20 at the city, 18 half way from Graymourne (`TierAt`); until the rebalance (phase 5) a monster past tier 5 fights at tier 5's strength.
- **Frame rate:** 8 to 31 a second in a play window of 1280 x 720 just after the city was built round her (not measured settled).

The city is built by Brokenspire's code, so what the user's lists gave the town is here too (the day and night, the sky, the weather, the music; the signs, the gardens, the ovens and food, the boarded windows' planks, the barracks' dummies, the panels' held keys; see [Graymourne's](graymourne.md#what-every-town-has)). None of these has been measured in Kethrys Spire: the tests measured them in Brokenspire.

## The tower's dungeon

- **Its halls by tier:** tiers 1, 2 and 4 the arcane halls (Mystic Dungeon's walls, its damaged walls for the rough ones, its stone tiles and a ring mosaic among the floors, its tiles overhead, all in darkened, greyed copies of their own stone: `MI_Arcane_Wall`, `MI_Arcane_Floor`, `MI_Arcane_Ceil`; "Arcane"), tier 3 the castle's halls ("Castle"), tier 5 both mixed; by every room's walls the tower's things (`ThemeProps`: ice crystals, candle stands, rune stones). Its torches and lamps burn violet (`DungeonLightTint` (0.5, 0.42, 1.0) at 0.6 of the way from their own colour). Only tier 1's first hall has been seen, and it keeps the castle's great-hall furniture (throne, carpet, banners) in the saved start section, as the other dungeons' do; in its own cream stone and the castle's orange light it read as Mistward Castle's great hall (the first run's picture), darkened and violet it does not (`test_ks6.json`'s).
- **Its casters** (`monsters.json`, five kinds among the castle's 56, weighted so that about two in three monsters of a tier are these): tier 1 the skeletal adept, tier 2 the bone witch (weaken), tier 3 the witch of the Spire (slow), tier 4 the lich of the Black Tower (poison, 1.25 times as big), tier 5 the elder witch (weaken, 1.1 times). Each shoots from afar (the castle's monsters that shoot: 8 m or 15 m, a bolt of Magic Projectiles Vol. 5's), then fights hand to hand. The skeletons are Mystic Dungeon's modular skeletons with its mage robes, hoods, shoulders and arms worn over them (four parts each); the witches the Warriors Pack's witch; all play Mystic Dungeon's own clips (idle, walk, attacks, hits, deaths), the meshes' skeletons made compatible with its mannequin. All five measured playing (their bones moving 0.6 to 5 cm in half a second of their idle, 20 to 100 cm walking, over four runs).
- **Its own record:** its tiers, its start set's loot, its bosses and prisoners and the quests' counters are kept under its key (`DungeonKey` "kethrys"), apart from the other dungeons'. No final boss. Its last tier's boss drops Heaven's key and map (the chain's sixth; Heaven is not built yet).
- **Her whole state** goes down and comes back with her.
