improvements with the current game
player can walk through the farm animals, such as the cows.
the signs outside buildings look like they're floating and they often fall outside of the sign's boundaries.  the font
should also be an appropriate medieval one for the town of brokenspire and vell Morath.
inside the inns, it is often too dark where the customers are sitting.
the windmill is embedded in the ground while the arms of the windmill turns, hitting into the ground.
there should be a door for the animal pens that can be opened and closed.
the cobblestone streets should be at most 3 people wide.
the ground is uniformly flat throughout all of brokenspire.  there should be some terrain that is above the 
ground level and some that's below ground level.  the houses and buildings should be on flat land, but unoccupied
terrain could rise and lower.
gardens can be lootable with ingredients that can be made into food at an oven.
if a table has food items on it, it should be lootable.
the portcullis at Brokenspire can be walked through and there are gaps on either side of it.  there should be a collider
that prevents someone from walking through it.  there should be a way to open the portcullis like a lever or other 
mechanism.
the sky is uniformly grey.  there should be clouds of different sizes and types, birds of different sizes and
types, the sun if it's daytime, the moon if it's night time, stars if it's night if there's no cloud cover
the transition between terrain types is too abrupt and looks like a straight line from above.  it should be a more
natural transition and it should be smooth.  the biome's name should also be visible under the mini map once the player
is in the wilderness.
all of the ground terrain in the wilderness is uniformly flat.  there should be natural hills, dips, ridges, berms, just like a real
wilderness.  
the wilderness should also have a lot more foliage than what it has now, which includes lots more bushes, grass that's 
blowing in the wind, trees of various sizes depending on what biome they're in
the road outside brokenspire is visible on the map, but it is indistinguishable and not visible in the wilderness
the different terrain types are in regular squares and grids on the map.  it should not be that regular or mechanical, 
it should look more natural and ragged and uneven, and the different biome type should gradually flow into each other instead
of abruptly changing and looking like a straight line on the map
when it's night time, there is no moonlight or starlight
the music should change every so often.  after 5-10 minutes, the music should change to another appropriate song for the 
scene the player is in
there should be more animals and insects that are appropriate for that biome.  those animals should flee from the player if 
the player gets too close (1-3 meters).
there is no wind of weather effects
there is no way to name your own save
