# Controls

[README](../README.md) · [Levels](../README.md#levels) · [Documentation](../README.md#documentation)

Each level's own keys are also in its document under [levels/](levels/).

The game is the packaged one (`pipeline/package_mistward.ps1`): the hero menu, Brokenspire and the wilderness round it with its three cities (Vel Morrath, Ashenreach, Graymourne), and their dungeons (Mistward Castle, the catacombs, The War Engine, the sewers). Its controls are below, the keyboard and mouse first and the gamepad (an Xbox controller) in brackets. The HUD's upper left lists the hero's own controls for whichever device was used last (the Options page's Controls hint hides it). The earlier levels' keys are at the end.

## The hero menu (`mistward_castle_select`)

- A / D, the arrows or the mouse wheel: another hero (the D-pad, LB / RB or the left stick); a click on a hero's name or on the arrows beside her.
- Enter, Space, a click on START or on her name again: start her, a new game in Brokenspire (A or Start).
- CONTINUE (C or a click; X): loads the newest save (shown when there is one).
- AUTOPLAY over START (P or a click; Y): the game plays her. [ / ] or the arrows beside it (D-pad down / up) choose how long: 1, 2, 5, 10 or 30 minutes, or until a key. Any key, click or push of a stick gives her back to the player (not with Alt or the Windows key held).
- QUIT GAME (upper right): a click quits; Escape (B) once asks, again within 3 s quits.

## Each hero's own

- **The Warrior:** left mouse strikes (RB); the right mouse button held blocks with the shield (LB held). While she blocks she cannot swing, the move's ahead and back (W / S, the stick up / down) walk her slowly along her own facing, whether she walks or runs, and its sides (A / D, the stick left / right) turn her and never move her.
- **The Mage:** left mouse or E casts the spell drawn (RB or LB); the wheel (Y) switches between fire, dark fire and the spells she has learned.
- **The Warlock:** left mouse cuts with her sword (RB); right mouse or E casts the fire alongside it (LB).
- **The Rogue:** left mouse strikes with the daggers or shoots the crossbow, whichever is in her hands (RB); the wheel (Y) switches them; B rolls, her uncanny dodge (B); Q hides her, her stealth, at the cost of dexterity energy, and Q again ends it early (LB, or its quick slot).
- **The Martial Artist:** left mouse strikes or makes the chi attack drawn (RB); the wheel (Y) switches between her fists and her chi attacks; B dodges (B); V or right mouse dashes into a strike (RT).
- **The Summoner:** as the Mage: left mouse or E casts the spell or summoning drawn (RB or LB); the wheel (Y) steps through fire, dark fire and her summonings.

## F: what it does (X)

F acts on the nearest thing in reach (a door or container nearer than anything else comes first); the prompt says what it will do.

- **Everywhere:** opens and closes doors and containers; takes up a pile: her own dropped things, a harvest from a kill.
- **In the dungeons:** opens prison cells; takes a teleporter's ring or teleports from a pad (G picks the tier on the great hall's pad); disarms a trap the Rogue has seen; a shrine's blessing, a fountain; at an anvil, the crafting panel. In Mistward Castle the arch in the great hall brings her back to Brokenspire.
- **In the towns:** talks to someone near (a quest giver: a panel of questions and quests; a keeper: a word and the building's service; anyone else: a line of theirs); uses a service where it stands (the trader's counter, the healer's basin, the temple's altar, the storehouse's chest, the smith's anvil, the teleport circle, the quest board); pries a boarded door's planks loose; opens and closes a pen's gate; pulls a lever by the gate (the portcullis rises or falls); harvests a garden (it grows again the next day); takes the food on a table; at a kitchen's bread oven, the crafting panel as THE OVEN.
- **In the wilderness:** takes a hunted animal's harvest where it fell.

Walked into, a city's way down opens its dungeon: the Mistgate in the Mistward Hall's back wall (Mistward Castle), the stair at Vel Morrath's cathedral's choir wall (the catacombs), the iron gate in Ashenreach's foundry (The War Engine), the round sewer gate in Graymourne's Sluice House (the sewers). Each dungeon's Escape menu has Return to its town.

## The inventory (I or Tab; Start)

It pauses the game.

- W / S or the arrows (the D-pad up / down, the left stick) walk the list; held, it goes on after 0.35 s, a line every 0.08 s. The mouse: the pointer chooses a row, a click on it uses it, the wheel scrolls the list.
- Q / E (LB / RB) or a click on a tab: the tab before or after.
- Enter or Space (A): uses the chosen line: drinks or eats, puts armour, a shield or a ring on or off, lights or puts out the torch, uses a teleport ring; on a repair item it lists what that mends, on a slash the weapons it can go on (Backspace or B back to the inventory).
- A weapon's, spell's or ability's line into a quick slot: its number key (its own slot's number again takes it off), or Y (the right mouse button) into the slot chosen with the D-pad left / right.
- T (X): takes a weapon's slash off.
- I, Tab (Start, B) close it.

## The anvil and the oven

F (X) at an anvil opens the crafting panel; at a bread oven the same panel opens as THE OVEN, with the food recipes only. W / S, the arrows or the wheel (the D-pad, the left stick) choose; Enter, Space, E or a click (A) makes it; F, Backspace, I or Tab (B, X or Start) close it.

## The town's panels

A service, a keeper or a quest giver opens a panel, which pauses the game. W / S, the arrows or the wheel (the D-pad, the left stick) choose; held, they go on after 0.35 s, a row every 80 ms. Enter, Space, E or a click (A) does it. A / D, the left and right arrows or a click on a tab (LB / RB, the D-pad left / right) change the list's tab (the store's, the storage's). Escape, F, Tab or Backspace (B) close it.

## The maps (M; Back)

- **In a dungeon:** the whole map as a see-through overlay you keep playing under. The arrows (the D-pad) scroll it, C (R3) centres it.
- **In a town:** the town from above. The wheel or + / - (the right stick) zoom it up to six times; a drag with the left mouse button or the arrows (the D-pad) move it; C (R3) shows the whole town again. While it is open the mouse, its wheel and the right stick are the map's.
- **In the wilderness:** the map of the world (in a town's walls the town's own), opened 4 km across round her. The wheel or + / - (the right stick) zoom it; a drag with the left mouse button or the arrows (the D-pad, the left stick) move it; C (R3) brings it back over her; F or Enter (A) travels to the found place under the cross (not while a monster is after her).

## The Escape menu (Escape)

No gamepad button opens it. W / S or the arrows (the D-pad up / down) choose a line, A / D or the left and right arrows (the D-pad left / right) change a setting, Enter or Space (A) presses a button. The mouse: the line under the pointer is chosen, a click presses it, a click or a drag on a volume's track sets it. Escape (B) goes back to the main page from another page, and on the main page closes the menu.

- **The main page:** Resume, Save the game, Load a game, Options, Choose another hero (a new game), Quit; in a dungeon also Return to its town, after Options.
- **Options:** Overall volume, Action sounds, Environment sounds, Music; Controls hint (on or off); Resolution; Display mode (fullscreen, windowed fullscreen, windowed); Apply display settings; Back.
- **Save the game:** the keys type its name (letters, digits, spaces, hyphens and apostrophes, 40 at most; each word's first letter a capital), Backspace takes off a letter and Delete the whole name; Up / Down (the D-pad) choose; Enter (A) saves, over the save of that name if there is one.
- **Load a game:** the autosave and the named saves, newest first, each with where and when; Enter or a click (A) loads one.

In the editor Shift+Escape stops play.

## The console (the Shipping game)

The console key (`, or the input settings' `ConsoleKeys`) opens it and shuts it, Escape shuts it; no gamepad button opens it. Enter runs the line; Up / Down bring back the lines typed before; Tab completes a command's name; PageUp / PageDown scroll; `help` (or `?`) lists the game's own commands (`rd.*`) with their values; `clear` (or `cls`) empties it. A command's name alone shows its value, a name and a value sets it. While it is open nothing typed or clicked reaches the game. In the editor and a Development game the engine's own console has the key (`rd.GameConsole 1` or `-RDConsole` puts this one on it).

- `rd.WildRunSpeed <x>`: her running speed in the wilderness outside the towns' walls, for testing: 1, 5, 10, 15, 20, 30 or 40 (another number takes the nearest). `-RDWildRunSpeed=<x>` on the command line sets it too.
