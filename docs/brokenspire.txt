this is for a new level called Brokenspire
the overhead map has a papyrus or scroll look with stylized icons that look like the actual buildings and labels
on those icons of what the buildings do.  the mini map will be like the dungeon mini map.
this will be the spec for a starting town procedural generation.  it won't be a static description:
the starting town is procedurally generated with .5km x .5km area at start with a starting town called Brokenspire
the starting town:
the terrain generation rules for Brokenspire should have the following with functionality:
    town square, smithy, general store or trader, healer / shrine, temple, somewhere the player
    can store equipment / crafting / etc, teleport circle that can be used when a corresponding
    circle is discovered in the wilderness, quest board
    mistward castle building that's the entrance to the mistward castle dungeon
    all town buildings and the castle should have functional interiors with working doors,
    containers, that can be opened and walked through
    in the castle there should be a central room that the player can walk through that puts them into the
    mistward_castle level.  this central room is guarded by a random assortment of 5-10 Tier 1 monsters who
    don't wander outside of that room
without functionality:
    tavern or inn, homes either burned down or boarded up or have people living in them,
    chapel, graveyard at the edge of town, a barracks with soldiers in it, market stalls,
    a well, a still, a mill, a wall, guard towers, a wall, a gate that opens out into the
    dangerous wilderness
townsfolk with functionality:
    elder, mayor, leader - gives opening quests, explains why he needs your help
    mysterious stranger / scholar - knows more about what kind of evil is happening
    guard captain - has quests that send you into the wilderness or into the local Mistward Castle dungeon
townsfolk without functionality:
    refugees, drunk at the tavern, children, a town idiot, doomsayer preacher, grieving parent or spouse
    guards, militia on watch
    gruff but kind blacksmith, sly/greedy trader, a tired healer, a witch or alchemist who lives at the edge
    of town
