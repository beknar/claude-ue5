the wilderness
the map of the wilderness has a papyrus or scroll look at the edges, but the geographical features are a
3d stylized canvas, with the biomes and terrain represented by their realistic top down look.  points of
interested are represented by stylized symbols, like villages, towns, major cities, camps, lone houses in
the wilderness, spawn points, ruins, ancient ritual sites, caves, monster lairs, etc.  the wilderness map
will have a fog of war that disappears when the player walks into that area.  the places and terrain that the
player encounters will be procedurally generated as he encounters them and revealed on the overhead map.
once the player reveals the terrain on the map by encountering them, that procedurally generated terrain
becomes a permanent part of the map, to be saved in the player's save file.  in order to save memory, once
the player gets far enough from a part of the map, that part of the map should de-spawn and its memory
deallocated.  the player should only be able to have an active amount of terrain in a 2km x 2km area of
the world.  for example, if the player moves 2km away from Brokenspire, Brokenspire is deallocated from
memory and only the 2km x 2km block the player is in is active.  this only applies to the wilderness, not
the town the player is in or any of the dungeons.
terrain procedural generation templates: grasslands, meadows, farms, roads, savannahs, hills, mountains,
tundras, plains, pine forests, temperate autumn forests, marshes, wetlands, mountains and peaks, volcanoes,
coastal regions, arctic regions, canyons, mesas, canyon networks, cold teppes, highland moorland, boreal pine forest,
lakes, rivers, ponds, temperate aspen, volcanic tundra, geothermal caldera, glacial, taigas, rocky coastal
there should be transition between biomes and areas.
refer to this picture for an example of aesthetics for the overhead wilderness map "E:\code\claude-ue5\ai models\overhead-map-example.png"
once a player discovers a point of interest, the player will be able to fast travel from one point to another.
Brokenspire is located on the mid-southern of the continent.  For now, there should be a marker for Vel Morrath located
to the east of Brokenspire, close to but not on the coast.  The marker for Ashenreach should be in the middle of
the continent, north of Brokenspire.  Graymourne should be on the mid northern coast.
when the hero enters a town, the overhead map and the mini map switch to the local town.
when all of the above are done, commit to a PR, review the changes, fix any errors, merge if it is mergeable.

Answers (2026-11-03):
- The continent: 300 x 300 km (built as written otherwise).
- Vel Morrath, Ashenreach and Graymourne: a marker and a small generated walled town at each (Brokenspire's generator,
  another seed); entering it switches the maps to that town.
- The save file: autosave in one slot, and Save / Load entries in the Escape menu.
- Fast travel: from the map, to any discovered place, not while in combat.
