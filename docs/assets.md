# Asset attribution

Every visual and sound asset of this project, and where it comes from.

The repository holds none of these assets: only scripts, source code and small data files. Packs, sounds and characters stay on the project owner's machine and are used under their store licences. The pictures committed under `docs/images` are renders made by this project.

- **Store links:** packs from the Fab store link to their listing (`https://www.fab.com/listings/<id>`, the id taken from the local Fab launcher data).
- **Sellers:** a seller is named where the local Fab data or the pack itself names one. Otherwise the listing is the reference.

## Made by this project

Made by the scripts in `pipeline/` and the C++ module, from the sources above or from nothing:
- the castle furniture kit (Blender, procedural);
- the meadow terrain and its textures (numpy);
- the studio cyclorama;
- the openable doors and containers cut from pack meshes;
- every material and effect built in Python: blood, wounds, reveal scan, monster recolours, sky, splashes;
- the fighter, kitbashed from the packs above;
- the procedural layouts, maps and HUDs;
- the demo-reel overlays;
- the README stills and the monster gallery's pictures.
